Returns & Refunds Policy

Returns are accepted within 6 to 7 days from the date of delivery, subject to product condition checks.

Once the returned product is received and verified by our team, the refund will be processed within 15 to 20 working days to the original payment mode.

For return requests, please submit your request from your account dashboard or contact support.
